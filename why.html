Why I built klk

My kids were about six or seven when they started noticing that my wife and I posted almost everything we did. We'd share with them who liked a photo they were in, or if family or a friend commented on something. They wanted in on the fun of posting something and seeing who liked it and who commented on it. (Early Instagram was just fun.) Obviously giving them actual Instagram accounts at that age would have been irresponsible and against Instagram's terms of service. So, I made kidstagram.

kidstagram only exists on the phones of people I've explicitly invited through Apple's TestFlight system. Just the moms, dads, cousins, and grandparents who care about them. It actually started with just the younger cousins so they could be as silly and annoying as they wanted (no grown-ups allowed!). It wasn't long before they started posting so many cute memories that the rest of the family wanted in. So I slowly started inviting more people to the testing group. Once everyone was invited, we had 23 members. You can't follow or unfollow anyone. You can't block anyone. Every post sends a notification to everyone in the app. It worked perfectly (and still works) for that audience.

Every so often I'd tell coworkers or friends about the project, and they'd say they wished they had something they could give to their kids, or just a way to share memories privately. kidstagram was so tied to my family that it didn't make sense to try to fit them into it. So, I took everything that worked well and started a new project.

Knowing that I'd have other people's photos and videos in a database I managed made me start with different priorities. It needed to be fully encrypted from me. I didn't want to be able to see a photo or video that didn't belong to me. It also needed to be fully encrypted from everyone else. I wanted people to be able to share something with their family that didn't need to go out to their friends too, or the whole world for that matter. klk was born. Not only was it a private island for anyone you shared memories with, but you could have many private islands. Sharing exactly what you want with whoever you want.

I hope someone finds it useful. I'd love for it to take off and to be the only thing I work on. Even if it didn't make any money and was just useful enough for people to use, it would still be fulfilling to work on.
