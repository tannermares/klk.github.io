Common questions

How do I join a klk?

You need an invite from the person who made it. Each invite works once, so it's good for exactly one person. There's no directory of klks to browse, and no way to ask your way into one you weren't invited to.

My invite says it's expired or already used.

Ask whoever invited you for a new one. An invite works once and expires after a week. If someone else opened it first, or it sat around too long, it stops working on purpose.

Who can see what I post?

Only the people in the klk you posted it to. People you don't share a klk with can't see your posts, or even find out you have an account. There are no public profiles, no explore feed, and nothing on the web. Not even I can see what you post. The privacy policy has the full picture, including what the server can still see.

Is klk end-to-end encrypted?

Yes. Photos, videos, captions, comments and profile photos are encrypted on your phone before they're uploaded, and only the phones of the people in that klk can open them. Not even I can see them.

What isn't encrypted is what klk needs to work: names, klk names, and who posted, liked or commented when. The privacy policy lists all of it, and How encryption works explains how it's done.

What's the recovery passphrase for?

It unlocks your klks when you sign in on a new phone. Your keys never leave your phone unlocked, so the passphrase is how a new phone gets to open what your old one could. I never see it and can't reset it. If you lose it, I can't get you back in, so keep it somewhere safe, like a password manager.

How much does klk cost?

It's free. There are no ads and nothing is sold.

How do I leave a klk, or delete one?

You can leave any klk whenever you want. What you already posted there stays in that klk, so delete anything you want gone before you leave. The person who made a klk can remove people or delete the whole klk, which deletes everything posted to it.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to your profile, then Settings, then Delete account. It deletes your account, your profile and everything you posted, and takes you out of your klks. Deleting your account has the full list of what's deleted and what's kept, and how to do it if you don't have the app anymore.

Something in a klk needs reporting.

Tap the … on a post, clip, moment or profile, or press and hold a comment, and choose Report. Since everything is end-to-end encrypted, a report sends me only the thing you're reporting and nothing else in the klk. I review reports within 24 hours, and I may remove the content and suspend the accounts involved when it breaks the terms or the law.

From the same menu you can Block someone. You stop seeing their posts, comments and moments, you stop getting notifications from them, and they aren't told. You can unblock them any time in Settings → Blocked people. You can also email support@klkapp.com.

Do photos keep their location data?

No. Before anything is encrypted, your phone re-encodes photos, which strips their EXIF data, location included. Videos are re-encoded without metadata too, unless they're already small and have no location, in which case they're uploaded as recorded. Either way, whatever's left is encrypted with the file, so only your klk can see it.

Still stuck?

Email support@klkapp.com with what happened and what you expected to happen. If it's a bug, include your phone and OS version and I'll take a look.
