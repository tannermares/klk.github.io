You're already sharing photos.

It's probably in a group text or a shared album. Both work fine right up until someone has an Android, or wants to say something about a photo.

Group chats are terrible. (Especially at photos)

One person on Android and the photos show up blurry, out of order, or not at all. klk works the same on iPhone and Android, so your Android friends don't get left behind.

Shared albums are bad at comments

They're Apple only, and the likes and comments are buried behind a tap. In klk the likes and comments are the whole point. That's what makes it feel like everyone's actually there.

One for family, one for the trip

Some klks stick around, like family. Some are just for a weekend, like a vacation or a wedding. Make one, invite people with a link, and pick which klks show up in your feed.

The people that matter.

No ads, no explore, no algorithm. There's no follower count either, since there's nobody to perform for. Your feed is the people in your klks, in the order they posted.

Most social apps want you scrolling through strangers' highlight reels and measuring your own life against them. klk doesn't have anywhere for that to happen. It's just moments as they happen, from people you actually know.

It's also private. Everything you post belongs to one klk and is end-to-end encrypted, so only the people in that klk can open it. Not even I can see it. Invites are single-use links, so nobody wanders in, and nothing is ever public by accident. Here's exactly what that covers, and here's how it works.

Posts, Moments and Clips.

Three ways to share, and all of them stay in your klk.

Posts

Up to eight photos and videos at a time, with likes and comments.

Moments

The quick stuff. They don't disappear after a day, so nobody misses something just because they didn't open the app.

Clips

Short vertical videos you swipe through, only from people in your klks.
