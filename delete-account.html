Delete your klk account

Last updated October 3, 2026

klk is a private photo and video app by Tanner Mares. You can delete your klk account and the data attached to it at any time, whether or not you still have the app installed.

How to request deletion

  1. From the email address you sign in to klk with, write to privacy@klkapp.com with the subject “Delete my klk account”.
  2. If you created a klk that other people are still in, say whether to delete it (and everything posted to it) or hand it to another member.
  3. We delete your account within 30 days and email you when it is done. If the request comes from a different address, we will ask you to confirm from the one on the account first, so nobody can delete an account that is not theirs.

An option to delete your account from inside the app is on the way.

What gets deleted

  • Your account: your email address, password and sign-in.
  • Your profile: your display name and profile photo.
  • Everything you posted: your posts, moments and clips with their photos and videos, captions, and the comments and likes on them.
  • Your activity on other people’s posts: your comments, likes and views.
  • Your place in your klks: you are removed from every klk you belong to, along with your copies of their keys and any invites you created.
  • Your devices and keys: your push notification tokens and the encrypted backup of your keys that your recovery passphrase unlocks.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Backups may hold copies for up to 30 days before they age out.
  • Crash and error reports in Sentry, which include your user id, email address and display name, are kept for up to 90 days and then deleted automatically.
  • Our email exchange about your request, as a record that we carried it out.
  • Other people’s content stays, including what they posted in klks you shared. It belongs to them. Neither can we reach copies another member saved to their own device.

Deleting some data without deleting your account

You can do most of this yourself in the app, at any time:

  • Delete any of your posts, moments, clips or comments. Deleting a post also deletes its photos and videos from our storage.
  • Leave a klk. What you already posted there stays visible to that klk, so delete it before you leave if you want it gone.
  • Delete a klk you created, which deletes everything posted to it.
  • Change your display name or profile photo in Edit Profile.

For anything else, such as your crash reports or a copy of your data, email privacy@klkapp.com from the address on the account. The privacy policy describes everything klk stores.
